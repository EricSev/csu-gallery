Landing Page Structural Comparison

ANA vs. Signal Mutual
Columbia Southern University Learning Partner Pages

Executive Summary

CSU's learning partner landing pages have the potential to turn employer partnerships into student enrollments by giving prospects a customized starting point. Today the learning partner landing pages are cumbersome to create and maintain.

Goal: Lower the time and effort to create and maintain learning partner landing pages.

Objective: Build a landing page builder for business units that streamlines collaboration with marketing by capturing detailed, structured requests while completing much of the production work upfront. The tool would guide users through module selection (partner benefits, lead forms, Why Choose CSU, video testimonials, tuition tables, FAQ sections, CTA banners), section arrangement, and content entry, with locked standard elements (welcome bar, header, footer, consent disclosures) applied automatically. Automated checks would validate content against CSU branding guidelines and copy compliance rules, with AI flagging issues before submission. Business units would deliver marketing a near-complete page rather than a request to interpret, reducing back-and-forth and shifting marketing's role from production to final review and publication.

Key Structural Differences

1. Content Depth vs. Trust Signals

ANA: Prioritizes program-specific content with a detailed list of 8 nursing degree programs and a comprehensive tuition comparison table. This approach serves prospects who are researching specific educational pathways and need detailed cost information to make decisions.

Signal Mutual: Prioritizes institutional trust signals with the "CSU by Numbers" statistics, accreditation logos, and value proposition cards. This approach serves prospects who may be less familiar with CSU and need broader validation before engaging.

2. FAQ vs. Video Social Proof

ANA: Uses embedded video testimonial for social proof. No FAQ section. Relies on the comprehensive content to preemptively address questions.

Signal Mutual: Uses FAQ accordion to address common questions about transfer credits, enrollment timing, and support contacts. No video testimonial.

3. Tuition Comparison Approach

ANA: Full comparison table showing CSU against 10 competitors (Chamberlain, WGU, Purdue Global, Rasmussen, GCU, SNHU, Walden, Capella, Phoenix, Herzing) with detailed cost breakdowns. Highly persuasive for cost-conscious nursing students.

Signal Mutual: Simple promotional banner directing users to SaveWithCSU.com calculator. Less detailed but cleaner visual presentation.

4. Regulatory Compliance

ANA: Extensive disclaimers section required for nursing programs including state authorization status, licensure limitations, NLN CNEA pre-accreditation disclosure, and career outcome caveats.

Signal Mutual: Minimal compliance requirements with only standard consent disclosure in the form.

5. Header Design

ANA: Two-tier header with dark welcome bar featuring partner name and CTA, plus logo bar below. More prominent partner recognition.

Signal Mutual: Single-tier header with logo and Apply Now button. Cleaner but less partner-specific.

Template Selection Guidance

Use the ANA pattern when:

  • The partner serves a specific profession (nursing, fire science, criminal justice)
  • CSU offers specialized degree programs for that profession
  • Competitive tuition comparison is favorable and verifiable
  • Regulatory disclaimers are required (healthcare, education)
  • Video testimonials from the target profession are available

Use the Signal Mutual pattern when:

  • The partner serves a general workforce (insurance, manufacturing, associations)
  • Prospects may choose from the full program catalog
  • Building broad institutional trust is the priority
  • FAQ-based objection handling is preferred
  • A cleaner, shorter page is desired
