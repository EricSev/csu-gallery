Maritime Safety Courses

OSH 2306 — Maritime Safety Standards

OSHA maritime standards, shipyard competent person requirements, and regulatory compliance for marine operations.

OSH 3313 — Introduction to Maritime Workers’ Compensation

Longshore Act fundamentals, claims management, and how safety programs drive workers’ compensation outcomes.

OSH 3314 — Special Topics in Maritime Industry

Confined space entry, hot work, crane operations, and emerging hazards in shipyard and terminal environments.

OSH 4314 — Materials Handling in Maritime Industry

Rigging, lifting operations, cargo handling, and equipment safety for waterfront and ship repair operations.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does this program prepare me for maritime safety roles?
Courses were designed with input from Signal Mutual member companies representing shipyards, terminals, and maritime contractors. Each addresses OSHA maritime standards, workers’ compensation integration, confined space and hot work operations, and materials handling.
Can I complete courses while working rotating schedules?
Yes. Courses are 100% online with no set class times. Most CSU students balance full‑time employment with coursework.
What’s the difference between the certificate and the concentration?
The certificate includes four specialized courses and can be completed in approximately two terms. The B.S. in OSH with Maritime Concentration includes those same four courses within a complete bachelor’s degree, preparing you for management roles and CSP eligibility. Certificate credits transfer directly to the bachelor’s program.
Is CSU recognized by the Board of Certified Safety Professionals?
Yes. CSU’s bachelor’s and master’s programs in OSH are recognized by the Board of Certified Safety Professionals (BCSP) as Qualified Academic Programs. Graduates meet educational requirements for ASP and CSP certification eligibility.
Can my employer pay for this program?
Many Signal Mutual member companies offer tuition assistance. CSU can coordinate direct billing and deferred payment options.
