The Workforce Challenge No Member Can Solve Alone

Maritime employers face a structural workforce crisis. Baby boomer retirements outpace skilled trades recruitment. Community colleges produce candidates, but not at the skill levels shipyards require.

Individual members can't invest in training when competitors poach graduates. The industry needs a collective solution.

Signal Mutual is coordinating that solution. By combining the resources of CTR (workforce operations), Columbia Southern University (educational credibility), and our member network, we're building a sustainable pipeline that benefits the entire Mutual.

Proof of Concept

Alabama Pilot: Austal USA / Electric Boat

100%
Pass Rate
86%
Completion Rate
10 Weeks
Program Length
2x
Cohort Growth

CTR partnered with Bishop State Community College and Alabama state workforce programs to train structural shipfitters for Austal USA's Electric Boat submarine module production. All trainees were CTR W2 employees from day one, eligible for WIOA wage reimbursement. The program achieved a 100% pass rate on shipyard competency testing by week 10, with graduates placed directly on the production floor. Success led to doubling the second cohort size.
