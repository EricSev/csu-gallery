Partner Type Definitions

LP Learning Partner Landing Pages

Traffic Source: Direct promotion from CSU and the partner organization

Channels: Newsletters, email blasts, conferences, internal meetings, partner websites, HR communications

User Context: Prospect has heard about CSU through their employer/association and is actively investigating the educational benefit

Conversion Goal: Lead capture via form submission or application start

Current Partners

ANA IAFC Signal Mutual Delta FedEx NAEMT NVFC NYPD

Future Partners

Live View Technology StarCom CTR ADTC NCCER

CP Channel Partner (Benefit Company) Landing Pages

Traffic Source: Benefit platform portals and their marketing surfaces

Channels: Employee benefit portals, perks marketplaces, benefit company newsletters, HR system integrations

User Context: Employee browsing available perks, may not have prior awareness of CSU, comparing multiple education options

Conversion Goal: Quick conversion with clear value proposition and simple path to action

Current Partners

BenefitHub CoreStream Paychex (via CoreStream) EBG/TicketsatWork Reward Gateway Scholarship America Perkspot Access Development Eplee & Associates

Landing Page Builder: Template Selection Logic

Question 1: What type of partner is this?

Learning Partner → Organization with direct CSU partnership (employer, association, union)

Benefit Company → Platform aggregating employee perks for multiple employers

Question 2 (Learning Partners only): Is this a profession-specific partner?

Yes (nursing, fire, EMS, law enforcement) → Enable: Degree Programs List, Scholarship Highlight, Tuition Table, Disclaimers

No (general workforce) → Use standard LP template with Benefits Card + Form

Question 3: Does the partner have special pricing tiers?

Yes → Enable: Tiered Pricing Display

No → Use standard discount mention in Benefits Card

Question 4: Is a partner-specific scholarship available?

Yes → Enable: Scholarship Highlight module

No → Mention "Exclusive Scholarship Opportunities" in Benefits Card
