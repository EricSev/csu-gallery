The Workforce Challenge No Member Can Solve Alone

Maritime employers face a structural workforce shortage. Retirements outpace skilled trades recruitment. Community colleges graduate candidates, but most require extensive onboarding. Individual members hesitate to invest in training when competitors can hire their graduates.

Signal Mutual coordinates the collective solution. With CTR (operations), Columbia Southern University (education), and the Signal member network, we are building a sustainable pipeline that benefits the entire Mutual.

Three Partners. One Solution.

Signal Mutual — Initiative Leader

Engages members, validates safety curriculum, pursues government funding, and enforces program quality.

Columbia Southern University — Educational Partner

Provides accredited credentials, safety curriculum development, community college partnerships, and labor‑to‑leader degree pathways.

CTR Group — Operational Partner

Recruits and hires, delivers training, carries payroll during training, absorbs employment risk, and guarantees placement.

How the Model Works

1) CTR Recruits & Hires

Candidates are CTR W‑2 employees from day one.

2) 10‑Week Training

Employer‑specific skills and Signal‑validated safety curriculum. Paid training.

3) Competency Validation

Graduates pass shipyard testing standards before deployment.

4) Member Deployment

One‑year evaluation period as CTR employees before permanent hire.

Why This Works for Members

  • Zero training risk: CTR covers all costs during training and the first year of employment. Members pay standard contracted labor rates.
  • Pre‑validated skills: 100% pass rate in pilot program.
  • Try before you hire: Evaluate for one year as CTR employees.
  • Net new workers: Grows the labor pool instead of reshuffling talent.
  • Safety‑trained: Signal‑validated safety curriculum with OSHA 10/30.
  • Government funded: State workforce development programs cover training costs; Alabama WIOA reimburses 50% of wages for eligible hires.

Cohort Growth

CTR partnered with Bishop State Community College and Alabama state workforce programs to train structural shipfitters for Austal USA’s Electric Boat submarine module production. Trainees were CTR W‑2 employees from day one and eligible for wage reimbursement. All graduates met shipyard competency standards by week 10 and were placed directly on the production floor. Success led to doubling the second cohort.
